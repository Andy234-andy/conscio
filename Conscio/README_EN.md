# Conscio 🧠

**"You shouldn't have to choose information. Only the information worth being chosen should be there."**

Conscio is a **context-aware digital navigator** that lives in your Mac's menu bar.
It understands the app and document on your screen in real time and quietly surfaces exactly what you need. And it protects the moments you are deeply focused.

## ✨ Core Features

### 1. Real-time Context Engine
Monitors your frontmost app and open document every 2 seconds to understand what you're doing right now.

### 2. Smart Suggestion
Context-relevant references, documents, and tasks appear automatically. "Where was that file?" disappears.

### 3. Intelligent Noise Suppression (Focus Guard)
While you work in focus apps (Xcode, Safari, Pages...), notifications are silently held. A summary arrives once you're free.

### 4. Digital Habit Report
Focus time, context-switch frequency, and app usage stats — visualized positively. No scolding, just encouragement.

## 🚀 Getting Started

```bash
swift build
open Conscio.app   # pre-built bundle
```

- Look for the 🧠 icon in your menu bar
- Grant Accessibility permission on first launch for full precision

## 💡 Why it matters

Modern knowledge workers switch context hundreds of times a day. Task managers keep multiplying, yet we feel MORE managed than managing.

Conscio's philosophy is simple:
**Tools should move toward people — never the other way around.**
