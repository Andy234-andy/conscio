# 🚀 Product Hunt Launch Kit (EN)

## Product Name
**Conscio — Context-aware menu bar navigator for macOS**

## Tagline (40 chars max)
**"Your Mac finally understands what you're doing."**

Alt: "Let your Mac protect your focus."

## Short Description
Conscio lives in your menu bar, understands what app and document you're working with in real time, quietly surfaces the files and references you need, and silences notifications while you're deep in focus.

## Long Description
Modern knowledge workers switch context *hundreds* of times a day. We have more task managers than ever — and somehow feel MORE managed than managing.

Conscio flips the model: **tools should move toward people, never the other way around.**

✨ What it does:
- 🧠 **Real-time Context Engine** — watches your frontmost app & document every 2 seconds
- 💡 **Smart Suggestions** — relevant files, docs & references appear before you even search
- 🛡️ **Intelligent Noise Suppression** — notifications go silent during focus; a digest arrives when you surface
- 📈 **Digital Habit Report** — focus time and context stability, visualized positively (it never scolds)

Built with Swift/SwiftUI. 1.5 MB. No account. No cloud. Runs entirely on-device.
